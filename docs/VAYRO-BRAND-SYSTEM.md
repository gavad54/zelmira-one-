# VAYRO SIGNATURE — Brand system

## Brand core
- Master brand: VAYRO / وایرو
- Central platform: VAYRO ONE / وایرو وان
- Brand promise: Intelligence. Without limits. / هوشمندی، بدون مرز
- Positioning: a governed ecosystem of independent specialist digital products, connected through shared identity, licensing, permissions and auditable integrations.

## Existing independent product names retained
LEADORA, ARIANA, ARIA and ARMIN MARKET remain unchanged and independent. In particular, ARIA is not an accounting or ERP product; accounting and ERP belong to ARIANA.

## Visual direction
- Dark graphite / midnight navy foundations.
- Restrained champagne gold for brand authority.
- Ice blue for technology, connectivity and interactive accents.
- Geometric V monogram and orbital / connected-system motifs.
- High contrast, legible Persian typography, RTL-first layouts and mobile-first responsive behavior.
- Avoid excessive glow, decorative noise and tiny low-contrast labels.

## Product status language
A name or product card in VAYRO ONE does not imply that the underlying product has shipped. Until each product passes its own engineering, security, privacy, operational and UAT gates, label it as an ecosystem product or planned capability—not available, live or production-ready.

## Legal and market validation
All names are approved as working brand decisions within this project only. Trademark clearance, company-name search, domain and social handle availability, app-store collision checks and target-market linguistic review remain open. Do not claim legal availability or registration without evidence.
