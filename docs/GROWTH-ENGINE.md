# NAMA & PAYAM — product specification

## 1. Product intent

**NAMA** creates campaign landing pages tailored to a defined audience, product, channel and customer need. **PAYAM** manages campaign attribution and a compliant customer-referral programme.

The Instagram reference demonstrates the direction: audience-specific landing pages, persuasive but truthful content, campaign fit and conversion improvement. It is a product reference, not proof of any third-party tool's capabilities.

## 2. NAMA workflow

1. Select a product (LEADORA, ARIANA, ARIA, ARVAND or another approved product).
2. Define audience segment, geography, language, offer, channel and conversion goal.
3. Generate a page brief and copy variants with the AI assistant.
4. Require human approval for claims, pricing, guarantees, regulated categories and published content.
5. Preview Persian RTL and English LTR mobile/desktop layouts.
6. Publish to an approved domain with consent and privacy notices.
7. Measure visits, qualified leads and completed customer actions.
8. Run controlled A/B tests; do not fabricate testimonials, scarcity, endorsements or performance claims.

## 3. NAMA core capabilities

- Reusable responsive templates and product-specific design tokens.
- Audience segments and localized content.
- SEO metadata, social preview and campaign tags.
- Form validation, consent records and anti-spam protections.
- Analytics with data minimization and retention controls.
- A/B testing with documented hypotheses and statistically responsible interpretation.
- Version history, approval workflow, rollback and audit events.
- Exportable campaign report.

## 4. PAYAM referral programme — compliant alternative to pyramid recruitment

The product will support referrals tied to real, verified customer transactions, not payment for recruitment.

Mandatory controls:
- No joining fee, paid rank, mandatory starter kit or required inventory purchase to become a referrer.
- No reward merely for recruiting another referrer.
- Reward eligibility is based on a verified sale to an actual end customer, after refunds and chargebacks windows.
- Prefer one-level referral rewards at launch. Any multi-level commission must be disabled by default and enabled only after written jurisdiction-specific legal approval.
- If a lawful multi-level programme is approved, levels, rates, caps, qualifying retail-sale requirements, refund clawbacks and disclosure must be explicit and auditable.
- No income guarantees, misleading earnings examples or pressure tactics.
- Clear customer pricing, cancellation, refund and complaint channels.
- Detect self-referrals, duplicate accounts, circular transactions, fake orders and collusion.
- Maintain immutable attribution and reward ledger events; never edit balances directly.
- Separate pending, approved, reversed and paid reward states.
- Payouts require identity/tax checks appropriate to jurisdiction and a supported regulated payout provider.
- Publish programme terms and provide a plain-language earnings disclosure.

## 5. Shared engine, product-specific policies

The referral and campaign engine is shared infrastructure, but each product owns its eligibility policy and customer journey.

| Product | Referral event eligible for review | Disallowed shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| LEADORA | Verified subscription or eligible software sale | Reward for adding distributors alone |
| ARIANA | Verified license or support subscription | Reward for recruiting accountants alone |
| ARIA | Completed eligible service/listing transaction where legally permitted | Reward for creating accounts or posting listings alone |
| ARMIN MARKET | Verified paid supplier service, if introduced later | Reward for inviting suppliers without a real sale |
| ARVAND | Verified paid software/service contract | Reward for recruiting exporters/importers alone |
| GANJINEH | Completed eligible digital product purchase | Reward for paid membership recruitment |
| NEGAH | Completed creative service order | Reward for recruiting creators alone |
| SEPEHR | Verified subscription payment | Reward for adding seats without genuine usage |

No reward is generated by default. Product owners must configure a documented qualifying event, rate, cap, refund window, jurisdiction and approval workflow before activation.

## 6. Licensing and customer control

- Each specialist app is sold/licensed separately.
- A customer can buy one app without being forced to buy the rest of the suite.
- Tenant data is isolated and exportable.
- Subscription entitlement, support term, renewal date and license status are transparent.
- Platform software ownership and customer data/control rights are distinguished in contracts.
- No cross-tenant access or marketing use of customer data without a lawful basis and explicit permission.

## 7. Metrics

NAMA: qualified conversion rate, form completion, cost per qualified lead, page performance and opt-in rate.

PAYAM: verified referred sales, refund-adjusted revenue, reward liability, fraud rate, complaint rate, participant retention and share of sales to genuine end customers.

Do not optimize for raw recruitment counts or downline size.

## 8. Release gates

Before public launch: legal review by target jurisdiction; privacy impact review; transparent terms; consent and deletion flows; fraud controls; refund/chargeback handling; ledger reconciliation; payout-provider review; security tests; accessibility and mobile UAT. Until then, keep referral rewards in design/test mode and do not accept funds or promise earnings.
