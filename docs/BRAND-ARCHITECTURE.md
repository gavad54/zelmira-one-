# VAYRO ecosystem — naming architecture

## Master brand
- **VAYRO / وایرو** — parent application, platform and central governance identity.
- **ZELMIRA ONE** — historical project name only; do not display it in product UI, account flows, app icon or release artifacts.
- Brand line: **INTELLIGENCE. WITHOUT LIMITS.**
- Persian line: **هوشمندی، بدون مرز**
- Visual system: VAYRO SIGNATURE — dark luxury, restrained gold and ice-blue accents, Persian-first RTL and mobile-first layouts.

## Direct VAYRO sub-applications

| Domain | Product name | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Command center | DANA | Independent command center, AI analysis and governed orchestration |
| Accounting and ERP | ARIANA | Accounting, finance, tax, budgeting and enterprise resource planning |
| Distribution operations | LEADORA | Sales, distribution, inventory and commercial operations |
| Supplier request marketplace | ARMIN MARKET | Buyer requests and supplier quotations |
| Social and services | ARIA | Community, listings, services and business modules; no accounting or ERP |

## Preserved ARIA modules

| Legacy module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| ARVAND | Export/import workflows, documentation and partner discovery |
| CHARSU | Marketplace and commerce network |
| GANJINEH | Software and digital product storefront |
| NEGAH | Creative production and brand assets |
| SEPEHR | Storage, cloud and managed workspace capabilities |
| NAMA | Audience-specific campaign pages and conversion analytics |
| PAYAM | Campaign planning, attribution and compliant referrals |

The seven legacy module identities remain preserved under ARIA. Their names, keys, existing routes and functional scope are not deleted. New implementation work must preserve their domain behavior and maintain backward-compatible API filters where those keys are already used.

## Shared platform services

| Domain | Name | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Shared identity and licensing | VAYRO CORE | Authentication, tenant boundaries, license entitlements and audit |
| Product integration | VAYRO CONNECT | Scoped product APIs, event contracts and service identity |
| Platform governance | VAYRO ADMIN | Product registry, owner operations, policy and release controls |

Shared platform services do not create shared ownership of product-private data. Single sign-on, navigation and consolidated reporting do not merge product databases or business rules.

## Commercial ownership

Each direct sub-application is separately licensed. ARIA owns the seven preserved module domains; module-level entitlements may be introduced without reclassifying the modules as separate direct products. The customer receives the contracted usage rights and control over its own tenant data, exports and authorized users. The platform owner retains ownership of the software and intellectual property unless a separate written transfer agreement says otherwise. "100% under customer control" means customer control of its tenant, data and access—not transfer of source-code copyright or ownership of the whole platform.

## Financial and transaction boundary

For buyer/seller transactions between customers, the marketplace is a technology intermediary only where contracts and applicable law support that role. The platform must not claim zero liability categorically. Terms, payment handling, dispute processes, consumer law, product safety, tax and jurisdiction-specific duties require legal review. Avoid holding customer funds; if payments are later supported, use a regulated payment provider and explicit contractual allocation of responsibilities.
