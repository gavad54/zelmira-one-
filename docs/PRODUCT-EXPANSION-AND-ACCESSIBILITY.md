# VAYRO — Product Expansion and Inclusive UX

## Audience
The platform must be usable by first-time smartphone users, people with low digital literacy, older adults, professionals and technical specialists. Age-appropriate safeguards apply to younger users.

## Interaction modes
- Guided mode: one task per screen, plain-language copy, examples and safe defaults.
- Standard mode: familiar navigation, search, saved items and dashboards.
- Expert mode: filters, bulk operations, API and advanced settings behind clear permission checks.
- Voice assistance: optional speech-to-text and text-to-speech, with visible transcript, language selection and confirmation before submitting or acting.
- Low-bandwidth mode: compressed media, deferred nonessential requests, resumable uploads and safe offline drafts.

## Accessibility requirements
- WCAG 2.2 AA target.
- Text scaling without layout breakage.
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus.
- Screen-reader labels and semantic landmarks.
- Captions/transcripts for audio/video.
- Color is never the only status indicator.
- Reduced-motion preference and no flashing effects.
- Touch targets sized for mobile use.
- Error messages explain the correction in plain language.

## Search and discovery
- Search across product catalogue and authorized content only.
- Filters by category, region, language, accessibility and verified status.
- Explain ranking factors and allow chronological/relevance alternatives.
- Clearly label sponsored placements.
- Prevent paid placement from impersonating organic trust or safety status.

## Mashhad and Iranian identity
- Persian RTL and Persian editorial typography are primary.
- Optional motifs inspired by Mashhad's turquoise tilework, geometric patterns and architectural rhythm.
- Do not imply official affiliation with public or religious institutions.
- Provide local calendar and regional formatting preferences.
- Regional services must display verification source, last-updated time and provider responsibility.

## Product expansion governance
A new domain requires: user problem statement, domain expert, target users, data classification, legal review, abuse cases, accessibility review, service owner, operating cost, support model and measurable acceptance criteria.

## Never ship as a fake feature
Every control must either work, be explicitly disabled with an explanation, or be clearly labeled as planned. No fabricated AI answers, fake availability, invented providers, fake analytics or simulated payment status.
