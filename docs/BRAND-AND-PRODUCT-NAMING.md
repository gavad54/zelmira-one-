# VAYRO Brand and Product Naming

## Master brand and application name
- Master brand: **VAYRO / وایرو**
- User-facing application name: **وایرو**
- Do not display “VAYRO ONE”, “ZELMIRA ONE” or “ZELMIRA” as the application name.
- Brand line: **INTELLIGENCE. WITHOUT LIMITS.**

## Independent products
- LEADORA: distribution, sales, inventory and commercial operations.
- ARIANA: accounting, finance and ERP.
- ARIA: social, services and marketplace. It must not contain accounting or ERP.
- ARMIN MARKET: buyer requests and supplier quotations.

## Brand governance
These are working product identities, not proof of trademark availability. Before public commercial use, perform trademark clearance in target markets, domain and social-handle checks, linguistic review, and legal review. No name may be represented as registered or legally cleared until documented evidence exists.
